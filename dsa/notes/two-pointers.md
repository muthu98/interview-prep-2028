# Two Pointers

## Definition

Two Pointers uses two indices to solve problems efficiently in a single traversal.

---

## When to Use

- Sorted arrays
- Pair problems
- Maximum/Minimum values
- Buy & Sell problems
- Palindrome checking

---

## Types

### Opposite Direction

```
left -------- right
```

Examples

- Two Sum II
- Container With Most Water

---

### Same Direction

```
left right →

left moves only when needed
```

Examples

- Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

---

## Sliding Window vs Two Pointers

| Sliding Window | Two Pointers |
|---------------|--------------|
| Maintains a window | Tracks two positions |
| Window expands/shrinks | Pointers move independently |
| Subarray/Substring | Pair/Min-Max Tracking |
