# Behavioral Interview Preparation

## Day 20 — Tell Me About Yourself

Aim for 60–90 seconds: experience and stack → current role → one measurable impact → next-role goals.

### Practice Version

> Hi, I'm Muthukumar. I have around 7.5 years of experience as a full-stack developer, primarily working with React, React Native, Node.js, and TypeScript, with some exposure to Java.
>
> Currently, I work at NatWest as a frontend developer in the banking domain, contributing to a trading application used for currency-related workflows.
>
> In previous roles, I've worked on full-stack development, mobile applications, and platform engineering. At Walmart, I helped improve CI/CD and release automation, reducing desktop release time from around 8 hours to 2 hours and mobile release time from about 72 hours to 24 hours.
>
> Outside my day-to-day work, I continue building my skills in DSA, system design, AI engineering, and backend development.
>
> I'm looking for a role where I can take more ownership, solve larger technical problems, and grow toward a senior software engineering position in a strong product-based environment.

### Refinements

- Remove fillers such as "pretty much" and "kind of."
- Keep present tense for the current role and past tense for earlier impact.
- Mention upskilling once rather than repeating it.
- Use the Walmart figures as the concrete impact example.
